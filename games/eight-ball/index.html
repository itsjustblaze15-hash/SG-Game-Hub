How to play

Aim and shoot
Point at the table to aim. Click once to lock the angle while you adjust power. Set power and select Shoot, or pull back along the cue with the mouse and release. On touch, drag to aim and use Shoot.
Fine adjustments
Use the left and right arrows to aim and hold Shift for finer movement. Use the up and down arrows or scroll on the table to change power. Space shoots. Drag the spin dot for follow, draw, or sidespin.
Take your group
The table stays open after the break. The first legally potted ball after the break assigns solids or stripes. Hit your group first and then pot a ball or drive a ball to a cushion.
Fouls and the 8 ball
A foul gives your opponent ball in hand. Clear your group, then select a pocket or press its number from 1 to 6. Pocketing the 8 early, in the wrong pocket, or with a foul loses the rack. An 8 on the break is spotted.
Levels and credits
Finish CPU racks to earn XP. A win earns 100 XP and a loss earns 50 XP. Unlock equipment every five levels. In a staked rack the winner collects both stakes. Win a free rack to earn 25 credits. All credits are play money.
Practice
Clear the whole table at your own pace. Undo restores your last practice shot, including any balls that went in.
